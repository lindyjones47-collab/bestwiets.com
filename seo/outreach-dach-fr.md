**Why this batch matters:** GSC shows real German demand (519 impressions, position 22) and Austria at 23% CTR — but the brand ranks only #5 for its *own* name. That's an **authority/backlinks** gap. Content is now in place (country hubs + city pages); links are what move the needle.

## How to use this (read first — 2 min)

1. **Send in the site's own language.** German pitch → `.de`, `.at`, `.ch` sites. French pitch → `.fr` and French-Swiss/Belgian sites.
2. **Link the right page per target country** (this is the whole point):
   | Target site country | Link this page in your guest article |
   |---|---|
   | Germany (.de) | `bestwiets.com/de/cannabis-kaufen-deutschland` |
   | Austria (.at) | `bestwiets.com/cannabis-kaufen-oesterreich` |
   | Switzerland (.ch) | `bestwiets.com/cannabis-kaufen-schweiz` |
   | France (.fr) | `bestwiets.com/acheter-cannabis-france` |
3. **Personalise the one bracket `[konkreter Artikel]` / `[article précis]`** with a real post from their site. Generic blasts get ignored; one specific sentence multiplies reply rates.
4. **Lead with the free article, not the link.** Editors say yes to value.
5. **One follow-up only** (template at the bottom). A third nudge reads as spam and can hurt the brand in these communities.
